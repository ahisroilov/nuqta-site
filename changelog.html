Changelog

What changed.

Every change to Motif, newest first - including what was taken out, and why.

Planned · no date yet

Next Planned

  • A Mac version.

November 20, 2026

Today in time order, and license keys

  • The Command bar lists today by the clock - tasks with a time and calendar events - then "Any time", with overdue tasks first and their day in amber.
  • Live chips under the input show where a new task goes, its timebox and its repeat. Tab sends it to the Inbox.
  • License keys: activate once, use on up to 3 PCs, move between PCs in Settings → License. Being offline never locks Motif.
November 18-19, 2026

A clearer calendar

  • The running session is a soft block with a breathing bar and "now"; the current-time line sits behind the blocks with the time in the margin.
  • Sessions of one task close together merge into one block, its edge split per session - no more lines across the text.
November 17, 2026

Timeline and repeating tasks

  • Timeline: every open task in one searchable list, grouped by date or by status.
  • Repeating tasks: daily, weekdays, chosen days, monthly, or every N days - typed in plain words or set in the task panel. Coming days show faded in Week and Month.
  • A new task composer, and one typeface (Geist) everywhere in the app.
November 16, 2026

An always-visible notch and Google Calendar

  • With nothing in focus, the notch shows what is next, "Plan today", or - after 18:00 - "Day done" with your total. It only changes its text; it never opens anything by itself.
  • The notch opens into one smooth island on hover.
  • Read-only Google Calendar: events appear in green next to your tasks, and any event can become a task.
November 10, 2026

The notch, and a new Month and Inbox

  • The floating pill became a notch, flush with the top edge of the screen. Drag it along the top, even to another monitor.
  • Month shows each day's tasks as small chips: done, dropped, planned and in focus.
  • The Inbox gained an Overdue group, quick actions on hover, and can collapse to a slim rail.
November 9, 2026

Steps and notes

  • Each task can have small steps; the next one shows in the notch.
  • Notes on each task, saved as you type, with clickable links.
  • The task panel shows the sessions you spent on the task, with their scores.
November 6, 2026

Sync, a calmer installer - and one feature removed

  • Google Drive sync through a hidden app folder in your own Drive. Several PCs can each have a task in focus.
  • A one-page installer in Motif's own colours: no questions, no admin prompt.
  • Removed: flashcards on breaks. Typing cards by hand was too much work and they were rarely used. Breaks are now simply for rest.
  • The streak became always on, without a switch.
November 5, 2026

Breaks, focus map, streak and medals

  • The Break window, with a breathing circle. The next session never starts by itself.
  • The focus map and an optional mood check-in that never affects your score.
  • Sessions pause automatically when the PC sleeps, locks or sits idle for 5 minutes.
  • Streak and 20 medals, rest days and rest tokens; the Welcome window and the app icon; Settings; the Windows installer.
November 4, 2026

The first version

  • The Command bar, one shortcut away from any app.
  • One task in focus, with a timer at the top of the screen and the switch question.
  • The automatic focus score.
  • The Task hub: an Inbox and a calendar by day, week and month, with drag and drop.
