Guide

How Nuqta works.

Everything you need, from the first install to the last shortcut. Nuqta is small, so this page covers all of it.

Install

After you buy, Polar emails you a link to the installer. Run it and press Install. It takes a few seconds, installs for your Windows account only, and never asks for admin rights. There are no questions to answer.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": until the installer is code-signed, SmartScreen can warn about new apps. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Nuqta then lives in the tray, next to the clock. Its icon is three dots in a row; the centre one turns indigo while a focus session runs.

Activate your key

The first time Nuqta starts, it asks for the license key from your purchase email. Paste it and press Activate. That is the only time Nuqta needs the internet to work.

  • One key works on up to 3 PCs.
  • "That key doesn't exist" - check for a missing character.
  • "This key is already active on 3 PCs, or it was refunded" - deactivate it on one of your other PCs first (see License and PCs).
  • "Can't reach the license server" - check your connection and try again.

On the first run, six short Welcome steps follow. You can replay them any time from Settings → Show welcome again.

Your first task

  1. Press Ctrl Shift Space from any app. The Command bar appears.
  2. Type what you want to do, for example Write the report ~45m, and press Ctrl ↵ to add it and start right away.
  3. The notch at the top of your screen now shows the task and a timer. Work.
  4. When you are done, press the shortcut again and Ctrl D - or point at the notch and press the check.

The Command bar

The bar shows the task in focus (with a Done button) and today's list in time order: tasks with a time and today's calendar events by the clock, then "Any time" - overdue tasks first, with their day in amber, then the rest.

Focus sessions

Starting a task starts a focus session - 50 minutes by default. The notch shows the time; its thin line fills as the session runs.

  • Pause with Ctrl Shift P, from the notch, or from the tray menu.
  • Automatic pause: when your PC sleeps, locks, or gets no keyboard or mouse input for 5 minutes, the session pauses by itself - backdated to your last input - and stays paused until you resume.
  • Timebox: past it, the notch time turns amber. The next time you open the Command bar it asks once: Ship it (S), +10 minutes (+), or Break it down (B) into smaller tasks. Esc decides later.
  • Switching: only one task can be in focus. Starting another asks what happens to the current one: Done (D), Not finished yet (L) or No longer needed (X). Esc keeps working on it.

Breaks

When a session's time is up, the Break window appears: a slow breathing circle and a countdown (10 minutes by default). Enter starts the next session, + adds five minutes, Esc closes it. The next session never starts on its own.

If you turn on the mood check-in in Settings (once a day or after each session), the break first asks how you feel, from 1 to 5. It is only for you; it never changes your focus score.

The Task hub

Open it from the tray (Open Task hub). It has three pages in the sidebar: Tasks, Focus and Settings.

Inbox and calendar

  • The Inbox holds tasks without a day, plus Overdue tasks left on a past day. Type in "Add a task" - the same ~45m and repeat phrases work. Press N to jump there.
  • Day, Week, Month (D W M). ← → move, T goes to today.
  • Drag and drop: drop a task on a time to plan it (it snaps to 15 minutes), on a day header or a Month day to plan it without a time, or on the Inbox to unplan it. Past days are history and refuse drops.
  • Finished sessions, dropped tasks and calendar events all stay visible, so the calendar is an honest record.

Timeline

Press L (or pick it from the "Tasks" title menu) for one searchable list of all your tasks, grouped by date or by status.

The task panel

Click any task to open its panel beside the pointer. There you can change its day, time and timebox, see the sessions spent on it, add steps (the first open step shows in the notch as "Next"), write notes - links become clickable - and start it with Ctrl ↵. Alt ← → moves it a day, Alt ↑ ↓ moves it 15 minutes. Delete is in the ••• menu, with Undo.

Repeating tasks

Turn on Repeat in a task's panel, or type a repeat phrase. Choose Daily, Weekdays, Weekly on chosen days, Monthly on a day of the month, or Every N days.

  • Only the current occurrence exists as a task. When you finish or drop it, the next one appears.
  • The Week and Month views show the coming occurrences, faded, up to six weeks ahead.
  • In the ••• menu: Skip to next and Stop repeating.
  • Monthly on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

Focus score and map

Each session of at least 5 active minutes gets a score from 1 to 5 when it ends. It starts at 5, and loses one point each for: more than 10% paused; each time you opened the switch prompt (at most two); leaving more than 20% early for another task; running more than 25% over the timebox. Finishing early with Done is never penalised.

Focus → Focus map shows the average score by weekday and hour (07:00-22:00) over the last 8 weeks. After 14 days with sessions it names your best two-hour window.

Streak and medals

  • A day counts after one finished focus session of at least 20 minutes (set 10, 20 or 30 in Settings).
  • A day starts at 04:00, so late-night work belongs to the evening it started.
  • Rest days (Sunday by default) never break the streak; focusing on one still counts.
  • Rest tokens: one for every 7 counted days, up to two. A missed day uses one automatically.
  • 20 medals, from 5 days to 1000. A new medal is shown in your next break - never during focus. Open any medal in Focus → Streak to turn it in 3D.

Google Calendar

Settings → Connectors → Google Calendar → Connect. Your browser opens Google's sign-in; Nuqta asks only for read-only access to your calendar.

  • Events from your primary calendar, a week back to six weeks ahead, appear in green in the calendar and the Command bar. They refresh every 15 minutes.
  • Click an event and choose Turn into a task to plan work around it.
  • Nuqta never changes your calendar. Disconnect removes the events from Nuqta.
While Google is still reviewing Nuqta's sign-in, Google may show "Google hasn't verified this app" during sign-in.

Sync between PCs

Settings → Connectors → Google Drive → Connect. Nuqta keeps your data in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive that only Nuqta can see; you will not find it among your files. Connect the same Google account on your other PCs and they merge.

  • Each PC can have its own task in focus at the same time.
  • Your devices are listed under Settings → Devices.
  • Disconnect stops syncing. Tick "Also delete Nuqta's data in Google Drive" to remove the copy there.

License and PCs

Your key works on up to 3 PCs at once. To move it to a new PC, open Settings → License → Deactivate this PC on the old one, then activate on the new one. Nuqta checks the key about once a month when you are online; being offline never locks it.

Uninstall

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Nuqta → Uninstall. Your tasks and history are kept in %APPDATA%\app.nuqta, so a reinstall picks up where you left off; delete that folder to remove them too. Deactivate your key first if you want to use it on another PC.
