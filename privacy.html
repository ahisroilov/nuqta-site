Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short

Nuqta keeps your data on your own devices. It has no server, no user accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing is ever sold or shared.

Data Nuqta stores

Your tasks (with their steps and notes), focus sessions, focus scores, mood check-ins, streak, medals and settings. They are stored in a local database on your computer and never leave it unless you turn on sync. If you connect Google Calendar, a copy of your upcoming events is also kept in that local database.

License key

Nuqta is sold through Polar (polar.sh), which handles the payment and emails your license key. When you activate the key, Nuqta sends Polar the key and your computer's name, so you can see and manage your activations. About once a month, when you are online, Nuqta asks Polar whether the key is still valid. Nothing else is sent. The key is stored only on your computer. Payments are handled by Polar under the Polar Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Drive sync

Sync is off by default. If you connect Google Drive:

  • Nuqta asks Google for one permission only: drive.appdata - access to a hidden folder in your Drive that only Nuqta can use. Nuqta cannot see, read or change any of your other files.
  • Copies of your Nuqta data are written to that folder so your other devices can read them. The data goes directly between your device and Google; it never passes through any server run by Nuqta.
  • Nuqta shows your Google account e-mail address in Settings so you know which account is connected. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • The sign-in key from Google is stored in your operating system's secure credential store (on Windows: Credential Manager), not in Nuqta's files.
  • Google stores and protects that data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Calendar

Google Calendar is off by default. If you connect it:

  • Nuqta asks Google for one permission only: calendar.readonly - read-only access to your calendar. Nuqta never creates, changes or deletes events.
  • Nuqta reads the events of your primary calendar from 7 days back to 6 weeks ahead (title, start, end and link) and shows them next to your tasks. The copy is kept only in the local database on your computer; it is not synced and not sent anywhere else.
  • The sign-in key from Google is stored in your operating system's secure credential store, separately from Drive sync.
  • Settings → Connectors → Google Calendar → Disconnect removes the key and the local copy of your events.

Use of Google user data

Nuqta's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Drive data is used only to sync your own Nuqta data between your own devices; calendar data is used only to show your events inside Nuqta on the same computer. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone, and not read by humans.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Connectors → Google Drive → Disconnect, with "Also delete Nuqta's data in Google Drive", removes the copy in your Drive.
  • You can also revoke Nuqta's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Nuqta and deleting its data folder removes everything from your computer.

Network access

Nuqta connects to Polar's license server (api.polar.sh) to activate your key and to check it about once a month. With sync and Google Calendar off, it makes no other network connections. With either on, it also connects to Google's sign-in, Drive and Calendar servers.

Children

Nuqta does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.
